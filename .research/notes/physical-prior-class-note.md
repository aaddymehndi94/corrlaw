# Physical priors interact with the declared polynomial class

These are elementary analytical controls, not new physical laws or novelty claims.
They concern exact agreement on the entire unit circle; numerical tolerances,
finite noisy observations and finite positivity probes do not provide that premise.
Write U(u,v)=u²+2v² and r²=u²+v². Coordinates are dimensionless as in benchmark B.

## What origin and positivity imply in degree at most three

If a polynomial P of total degree at most3 agrees with U everywhere on the circle,
then

    P-U = (r²-1)(a*u+b*v+c).

One direct argument: divide P-U by the monic polynomial u²+v²-1 as a polynomial in u.
The remainder is A(v)*u+B(v). At u=±sqrt(1-v²), for every -1<v<1, both values vanish;
subtracting and adding gives A(v)=B(v)=0 on an interval, hence both polynomials are
identically zero. Total degree bounds the quotient to a*u+b*v+c.

The origin condition P(0,0)=0 sets c=0. This alone leaves the odd cubic aliases in
the numerical origin-only control. But if P is also nonnegative on a neighborhood
of the origin, the origin is a differentiable local minimum, so its gradient is
zero. The gradient of P there is (-a,-b), forcing a=b=0. Consequently P=U within
this exact degree<=3 class. Coordinate parity is an alternative way to eliminate
these same odd terms; it was the additional prior chosen for the numerical sweep.
This argument is not an interpretation of a finite public-probe positivity pass.

## A degree-four counterexample with stronger priors

For any0<alpha<=1,

    P_alpha = U + alpha*r²*(r²-1)

agrees with U on the entire circle and at the origin, is individually even in each
coordinate, and is globally nonnegative. To see nonnegativity, U>=r² gives

    P_alpha >= (1-alpha)*r² + alpha*r⁴ >= 0.

Its gradient at the origin is also zero. It nevertheless differs from U away from
the circle/origin. At (u,v)=(1/2,0) with alpha=1/4, U=1/4 whereas P=13/64.
These values are exact rationals. The candidate is outside the degree<=3 finite
library used by the physical-prior control; its existence is a limit on broader
uniqueness claims, not a failure of that restricted numerical experiment.

These assumptions do not include full harmonic-potential structure or forbid an
additional length scale after restoring dimensional units. Imposing those stronger
physical restrictions changes the class again. The main benchmark is deliberately
generic dimensionless regression and must be described that way.

Verification planned in examples/physical_prior_algebra.py, with exact SymPy
expansion/division, origin/gradient, parity, circle remainder, off-circle values,
and the nonnegative lower-bound identity. The inequality argument above is analytic;
a sampled grid is not substituted for a global positivity proof.

## Series-capacitance ambiguity under several stronger priors

The F preparation u=v is different from the dimensional monomial cases. For
0<lambda<=1/3, the rational family

    H_lambda(u,v) = u*v*(u+v) / ((u+v)² + lambda*(u-v)²)

is positive for positive inputs, symmetric, homogeneous of degree1, zero when
either input is zero and the other is positive, and equal to u/2 on the diagonal.
It differs from the series reference H_0=uv/(u+v) away from that diagonal. It is
also increasing in each positive input. Writing r=u/v, the numerator of the
u-derivative after clearing its positive squared denominator is

    (1-3*lambda)*r² + 2*(1+lambda)*r + (1+lambda),

which is positive for0<=lambda<=1/3 and r>0. Symmetry gives the other derivative.
Thus units, positivity, exchange symmetry, zero-axis values and monotonicity do
not by themselves identify the series law from exact diagonal observations.
Additional physical information can exclude this family; for example its small-u
slope is1/(1+lambda), so the precise limiting ratio H/u->1 would exclude lambda>0.

This is a separate elementary analytical construction, not an equation claimed to
have been generated by the algorithm. No assertion is made that every member fits
the primary normalized15-node search cap. Actual accepted witnesses still require
their full class/fit audit. The example explains why stronger physical priors can
remain insufficient in a larger rational class without implying unrestricted
nonidentifiability for every bounded model class.
